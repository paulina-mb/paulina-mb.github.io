How I work

Listen first, then decide with data

When I was promoted to lead former peers with far more years in the industry, I started by learning from them, then made decisions on evidence.

Two 1:1s with each person

One on results, one on growth. People on my team moved on to technical, commercial and project roles.

Build what's missing

If the team is losing time, I find out why and fix it, even when nobody asked for it.

Stay until it's closed

A client once asked for a different manager. I kept helping him anyway, and he kept calling me for help.
